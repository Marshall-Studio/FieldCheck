Privacy & file handling

Files are read in this browser tab only. They are not uploaded to a FieldCheck server. Optional local preferences store column choices and your light/dark theme setting — never CSV row contents. CSV previews are reconstructed tables of imported values — not Excel/Google Sheets documents, and they do not preserve spreadsheet formatting. Optional privacy-friendly traffic analytics, if enabled later on the host, would see page visits only, not spreadsheet contents.
